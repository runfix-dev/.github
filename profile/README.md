<h1 align="center">runfix</h1>

<p align="center"><b>You built it with AI. We keep it safe, cheap and running.</b></p>

runfix looks after apps built with AI tools like Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor and Claude Code, for founders who don't write much code.

### What runfix does

- **Ask, see the plan, say yes.** Tell runfix what you need in plain words. It shows what will change, what could break, what it costs and how to undo it. Nothing happens until you say yes.
- **Fixes arrive ready to approve.** When something breaks, runfix finds the cause, writes the fix with a test that proves it works, and sends it to you to approve.
- **Proven security findings.** runfix checks whether your app's protections actually work, the way a stranger would try. A problem only counts as critical when it's proven.
- **One control panel.** Bot protection, spend limits and data access rules across Cloudflare, Vercel and Supabase in one place, each change with one-click undo.
- **Spend guard.** Live spend across your AI and hosting providers, spike alerts, a month-end forecast and automatic brakes.
- **Plain-English monitoring.** Uptime checks, error tracking and status pages that tell you what went wrong in words you can act on.

### Status

runfix is in development. Pricing will be pay as you go: no plans and no monthly fee.
